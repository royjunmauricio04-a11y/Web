Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

COMPASS helps students find schools and colleges that match their interests and keep track of the application requirements for each one. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it's kept safe.

1. What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, and password (your password itself is never stored by us; it's handled securely by Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile information — your grade level, school, Learner Reference Number (LRN), and the interests, location, and school-type preferences you choose during sign-up.
  • Usage information — colleges you've saved or requested information about, and your checklist progress, so the site can show it back to you next time you log in.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To match you with colleges that fit your interests and preferences.
  • To send you admission-related information when you request it from a college's page.
  • To save your progress (like your requirements checklist) between visits.

3. Where your information is stored

Your account and profile data are stored using Firebase Authentication and Firestore, both operated by Google Cloud. When you request information about a college, we send a one-time email through our own mail server — we don't hand your email address over to the college or to any third party.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage (not third-party ad cookies) for a few practical things:

  • Keeping you signed in between visits.
  • Remembering which checklist items you've completed.
  • Remembering whether you've responded to this site's cookie notice.

We do not use tracking cookies for advertising, and we do not sell any information to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Who can see your information

Only you can see your own profile and checklist. We don't share your personal information with other students, schools, or outside companies, except when you personally choose to send your email to receive information from a specific college.

6. Your choices

  • You can update your profile information at any time from your account.
  • You can request that we delete your account and associated data.
  • You can decline optional cookies from the banner shown on your first visit — this won't stop you from signing in, since that part is required for the site to work.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of COMPASS after a change means you accept the updated policy.
